## title

Privacy policy

## updated

Last updated October 29, 2026

## intro

The short version: your data is yours. We don't sell it, we don't use it for ads, and you can download or delete it at any time.

## scope-heading

Who this covers

## scope

This policy covers the Kinetta beta at login.kinetta.app and the website at kinetta.app, run by Ben Banks in North Carolina, United States. If you use a copy of Kinetta that someone else runs, their privacy terms apply instead.

## collect-heading

What we keep

## collect

**Your account:** email, username, display name, password (stored scrambled, never in plain text), and an optional photo and bio.

## collect-records

**What you add or import:** workouts, activity files, routes, heart rate and other measurements, food, recipes, weight, goals, plans and settings. Body details like height and age are optional and only used for your own estimates.

## collect-chat

**Chats:** Kit and food-log conversations are private to you. A Kit chat is deleted when you leave it. Kit can remember up to 12 short notes about your goals and preferences; you can see or delete them in Me → Settings → What Kit remembers, or turn memory off in Kit, and notes older than six months are removed. We don't keep food photos after they're processed. When you import a recipe link we keep the recipe, not the web page.

## collect-other

**Beta requests:** your name, email, device and message. **Technical records:** your login sessions, the device type they're on and the app version/build when a new iPhone sign-in supplies it, the device types you've signed in from (so we can optionally email you about a new sign-in), sign-in attempts, and a record of each request the app makes (which part of the app, when, whether it worked and how long it took, but never what you entered or sent). Your IP address is used for at most a day, to limit password guessing. These records keep accounts safe, help us support you and fix problems.

## health-heading

Apple Health and location

## health

The iPhone app reads only the Apple Health data you allow. It writes to Apple Health only if you turn on Share nutrition, and then only your daily food totals (calories, protein, carbs, fat and fiber). You can change this any time in the Health app. Kinetta uses location for the Track map preview and activities you record, including while the iPhone app is in the background or the phone is locked. The preview stays on your phone; only recordings you save are uploaded. Imported activities can also include routes.

## garmin-heading

Garmin

## garmin

If you connect Garmin Connect, Kinetta receives the activities you allow in Garmin's sharing settings: type, time, distance, pace, heart rate, calories, elevation and the GPS route. They show up in your own activity list, maps, progress and plans, and count toward your daily calories. They follow your normal sharing choice, like any other activity. Kinetta never sells Garmin data, never uses it for ads and never gives it to anyone else for their own use.

## garmin-ai

**AI and Garmin data.** Kit, the AI coach, can read your Garmin activities only after you turn on "Let Kit read my Garmin activities" in Me → Settings → Connections. This is separate from the main AI switch and from Apple Health, and it's off until you choose it. When it's on, Kinetta sends only the activities a question needs to the AI provider shown in the app (Google Gemini or Anthropic Claude). Turn the switch off at any time and Kit stops reading them. Garmin data, and anything made from it, is never used to train, test or improve AI models.

## garmin-control

To stop, tap Disconnect in Me → Settings → Connections or remove Kinetta in Garmin Connect. Kinetta then tells Garmin to stop sending data and deletes its sign-in tokens. Activities already imported stay in your account until you delete them, and deleting your account removes them.

## sharing-heading

What other people can see

## sharing

New accounts share new activities with everyone on Kinetta. You can change this to approved friends or only you when you sign up, in Me → Settings → Sharing & privacy, or each time you save a recording. You also choose which details, like heart rate, are shown.

## sharing-maps

Shared maps hide where a route starts and ends, but the rest of the route and its time can still hint at where you live or your routine. We strip location data from photos you upload.

## sharing-segments

**Segments and leaderboards.** A segment you create starts private to you. If you publish it, its name, description and path are visible to everyone on this Kinetta server, and publishing can't be undone. Leaderboards are opt-in: you appear only after you turn on Me → Settings → Sharing & privacy → Segment leaderboards, and then only with your best time from activities shared with Everyone, with a visible map and an outdoor, unassisted declaration. Garmin Connect activities are never shown on public boards. Turning leaderboards off removes your public times; you still see your own times privately.

## sharing-pictures

**Public Kinetta cards.** Cards start off. If you turn yours on, anyone with its random link can see your username and only the photo, sports and up to two fixed summary stats you choose. Selected sports and totals include Only me workouts. No real name, individual activity, food entry, map, place, weight or bio appears. Food summary stats show days with food logged, never what you ate or calories. Turning the card off or making a new link stops the old page and image immediately; pictures already saved by other people cannot be recalled. Opening a card does not make anyone a friend. Requests need approval, and card signups retain the person's request choice through email confirmation. Kinetta stores joined/pending counts, not a tracking history of page views.

Recap totals and calendars also include Only me workouts. Individual activity highlights use shared activities. You review the picture before sharing.

Joining a challenge is optional and does not make people friends. Participants see only usernames, permitted profile photos and aggregate challenge progress. Only me workouts count toward progress, without revealing their individual details. Finished totals continue to reflect late entries, edits and deletions. Food challenges share logging-day or protein-goal-day counts, never meals, intake amounts or personal targets. Leaving or removal hides the participant immediately; blocking hides people from each other. Challenge data is deleted 90 days after the final local end or an early stop. Milestone offers stay private and use only fixed activity achievements or cumulative food-logging days. Photo and username start off in milestone pictures. Sharing or dismissing marks an offer seen; old imports stay silent. Optional milestone notifications start off and send at most one a day. The Share button makes a picture of your own activity (map, time and pace) that you send wherever you like. It hides the start and end unless you turn that off. Once you send it, it's out of Kinetta's hands. Nobody can make a picture of someone else's activity.

## identity

Other members can find you by name or username and see your profile and accepted-friend count. Profile totals, sports and distance charts count all your finished workouts, including Only me and friends-only ones; only activities shared with that person are listed or can be opened. Your friends list is visible only to you; other people do not receive it or a mutual-friends list. Anyone who can see an activity can see its cheers and comments. Your food entries, weight, body details and private notes are never shared. Deliberately shared recap pictures and public cards may include only the food-consistency summaries you select. Run/walk presets and per-step interval results stay private even when you share the activity. Recipes are shared only with the people you send them to.

## ai-heading

AI features (Kit)

## ai

When you set up your account, Kinetta asks whether to turn on Kit's AI features. Kit is for people 18 or older, and you confirm that when you turn it on. You can turn them on or off at any time with the AI features switch in Me → Settings; when they're off, nothing is sent. When they're on, Kinetta sends only what that feature needs to the AI provider (Google Gemini or Anthropic Claude; the app shows which). For example, a food photo for an estimate, or your recent workouts when you ask Kit about training. It never sends your whole account or your password. Apple Health and Garmin data each need a separate yes before they're included (see Garmin above). You can change these choices in Me → Settings.

## provider-retention

The AI provider may keep requests under its own terms: see the [Gemini terms](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms) or [Anthropic's privacy information](https://privacy.claude.com/en/articles/7996866-how-long-do-you-store-my-organization-s-data). Don't include anything you wouldn't want them to see.

## providers-heading

Other services we use

## providers

Kinetta runs on its own server in the United States, not a cloud hosting company. **Cloudflare** carries traffic to the app, encrypted, including data from connected services like Garmin. **OpenStreetMap** supplies map images to your device. When you draw a segment on a map, the routing and elevation services configured by the operator (by default Valhalla/FOSSGIS) receive the map points through Kinetta’s server. Place search sends your typed query and map area to the configured geocoder (by default Nominatim), only when you press Search. These services see the server’s address and may log requests; Kinetta sends no account, workout times or health measurements. Do not include private information in place searches. Segment map drawing and place search are switched off on the hosted beta for now, so nothing is sent to these services until they are turned back on. **USDA FoodData Central** and **Open Food Facts** receive food searches and barcodes, never your account details. **Recipe websites** receive a request for the page when you import a link, also without account details. **Apple** delivers iPhone notifications if you turn them on. **An email service** sends confirmation and password-reset links, account safety notices, and replies to beta requests, which may include an invite link.

## providers-connected

Services you connect yourself, such as Garmin Connect or Health Auto Export, also follow their own policies. Kinetta keeps their keys or sign-in tokens on the server, uses them only to read your own data, and never shows them back in the app. Disconnecting deletes them and stops new imports; anything already imported stays until you delete it.

## admin

The person running the server can technically see its database. That access is used only to run the service, help you, keep it secure or meet legal duties. Ordinary account support shows status, counts, birth year/approximate age and Kit permission/consent status, without opening your private workouts, routes, food, other health records or conversations. An administrator can correct birth year and allow or block Kit access. Allowing access never supplies your own AI consent or permission to include Apple Health or Garmin data. An administrator reviewing a report can see its reported comment, current profile bio/photo, or the reported activity’s summary and attached photos. Review access and actions are recorded without copying that content into the audit log. If you make a data request, we keep its type, status, verification method and dates, linked to your account while it exists; correspondence and identity documents are not stored in this register.

## retention-heading

How long we keep it

## retention

Your data is stored in the United States until you delete it or your account. Beta requests are removed when you delete your account. Server logs are trimmed automatically; request records and sign-in history are deleted after 90 days. AI usage totals (model, amount processed and cost, with no content) may be kept after deletion to track spending. Account deletion clears the account link from data-request records. Closed data-request metadata is removed after 90 days when the register is checked; retry receipts for admin actions are removed after 90 days during later confirmed actions. Minimal admin audit records can remain after deletion without copying your account name, password or private content.

## backups

We keep the 14 most recent nightly backups on the server, plus copies made before updates. These are not separately encrypted, and there is no off-site backup yet. Deleted data can stay in a backup until that backup is replaced.

## choices-heading

Your choices

## choices

In Me → Settings you can change your details, sharing, AI and notification settings. Optional planned-workout reminders and weekly-summary alerts start off. They use generic text without personal measurements, food details or location, and make no AI call. Turn either off under Notifications. Limited attempt records prevent duplicates; regular cleanup removes these records after 90 days. They are included in your data export and deleted with your account. Your phone may delay or suppress alerts, and an alert already delivered cannot be recalled.

You can edit or delete any activity, food entry or weigh-in to correct it. Me → Settings lets you download everything, including original activity files, or delete your account. Deleting your account removes your data and files right away, except for the backups described above.

## security-heading

Security

## security

We use encrypted connections, scrambled passwords and per-account access controls. Settings → Signed-in devices shows device labels and sign-in dates, without IP addresses. Sign out one other device or all the others there; use Log out for this one. New sign-in emails are on by default for confirmed email addresses when mail is available. The first device is remembered silently; a new device type can trigger an email. Change this in Settings → Notifications. No service is perfectly secure; if a problem affects your data, we'll tell you as the law requires. Kinetta is a fitness and food tracker, not medical advice.

## age-heading

Children

## age

Kinetta is not for anyone under 16. Kit, the optional AI assistant, is only for people 18 or older: you confirm your age when you turn it on, and Kit stays off without that confirmation. A recorded birth year indicating someone is under 18 also prevents AI use, even if an earlier confirmation exists. If you think a child has signed up, email us and we'll remove their information.

## contact

Email [feedback@kinetta.app](mailto:feedback@kinetta.app). We'll never ask for your password, and please don't post private details in public GitHub issues. If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and tell you about any big changes.

## map-backgrounds

Map images currently come from OpenStreetMap’s public tile servers. They receive your network address and the map area you view, without your Kinetta account details, activity times, health readings or cookies. Hiding the start and end of a shared activity limits what other members see; it does not hide the area you view from the map-image service.
