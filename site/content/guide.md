## guide-task-activity-heading

Read a completed workout

## guide-task-activity-description

Open Me → Your activities and select a workout. Read distance and elapsed time with their labels; pace and speed use elapsed time. Moving time is shown separately when recorded. Maps, splits and heart rate depend on what the recording source supplies.

## guide-task-import-heading

Import an activity or investigate missing data

## guide-task-import-description

Check Settings → Sharing & privacy before importing. Choose original FIT, GPX or TCX files, including compressed .gz files, or a Strava export ZIP. In the iPhone app, Import history opens your browser. Keep your original recording.

## guide-import-check

In Recent imports, check what was added, detected as a duplicate or could not be read. Open Details for reported problems; “Uploaded” alone does not mean processing has finished. Compare the recorded distance and labelled times with the original. A source may omit GPS points. Where Add the map is offered, follow its original-file instructions. Keep the original and ask for help if a mismatch remains; avoid deleting or repeatedly reimporting history to guess at a repair.

## guide-task-meal-heading

Log a meal and review totals

## guide-task-meal-description

Open Nutrition → Add food. Search for food, scan a barcode, choose a usual food or enter known nutrition manually. Check each food and amount. Add to log asks which meal to save it to; review the saved entry and daily totals afterwards. Optional estimates require availability and consent.

## guide-task-week-heading

Revisit a training week

## guide-task-week-description

Open Progress → Totals. Check the sport, choose Week and select Distance or Time. Tap a dated bar for its total or switch to Table for distance, time, workout count and climbing. Choose Month or Year for a wider view. Totals describe recorded activities; a week still in progress is incomplete.

## guide-task-achievements-heading

Find achievements to earn

## guide-task-achievements-description

Open Me → Achievements. Open a track to see its named levels and progress towards the next one. Run distances keep their named units: 5 km, 10 km, 21.1 km and 42.2 km, then 50, 100, 250, 500 and 1,000 miles. After the final named level, the track continues at multiples of its last distance.

## guide-task-lifting-heading

Check your previous set and log the next

## guide-task-lifting-description

In Track, choose Lifting and tap Start. Add an exercise, review your previous load and reps, then record and complete each set. An unfinished session stays in Track. Progress → Lifting has exercise history and records.
