## recording-live-heading

Your pace as you go

## recording-live

See your whole-activity average and separate lap pace. Laps default to miles until you mark your own. Rides and skiing show speed. Expand the map while recording, then review charts and add photos before saving. Spoken mile/km updates can continue with the iPhone locked.

## recording-distance-heading

Distance and pauses

## recording-distance

A filter ignores small GPS movements while still and leaves poor fixes out of your total. GPS remains approximate. Auto-pause stops the clock after 15 seconds still and resumes when you move. It starts on for runs, walks and hikes, off for rides.

## recording-watch-heading

Phone and watch, one workout

## recording-watch

Record on both: when at least 80% of the longer workout overlaps, the imported watch copy and phone recording become one activity, with available watch heart rate. A short run inside a long hike stays separate.

## insights-heading

## insights-intro

Recording a workout is the easy part. Kinetta helps you understand the trend behind it, using only your own records.

## insights-load-heading

Fitter, or just tired?

## insights-load

Fitness and freshness turn every workout into a running picture of your training load. You'll see when you're building, when you're due an easy day, and when a week jumped too fast.

## insights-route-heading

Faster on your favorite loop?

## insights-route

Run a route again and Kinetta lines it up against every earlier effort: your pace, your heart rate, and whether you're getting more pace for each heartbeat.
