Training

Runs, rides, walks and lifting.

Bring in workouts from Apple Watch, Garmin or any watch file, follow a running plan and track lifting sets. Then see how it's really going, week to week and route to route.

Record with your phone.

Your pace as you go

See your whole-activity average and separate lap pace. Laps default to miles until you mark your own. Rides and skiing show speed. Expand the map while recording, then review charts and add photos before saving. Spoken mile/km updates can continue with the iPhone locked.

Distance and pauses

A filter ignores small GPS movements while still and leaves poor fixes out of your total. GPS remains approximate. Auto-pause stops the clock after 15 seconds still and resumes when you move. It starts on for runs, walks and hikes, off for rides.

Phone and watch, one workout

Record on both: when at least 80% of the longer workout overlaps, the imported watch copy and phone recording become one activity, with available watch heart rate. A short run inside a long hike stays separate.

Your numbers, explained

Know how your training is really going.

Recording a workout is the easy part. Kinetta helps you understand the trend behind it, using only your own records.

Fitter, or just tired?

Fitness and freshness turn every workout into a running picture of your training load. You'll see when you're building, when you're due an easy day, and when a week jumped too fast.

Faster on your favorite loop?

Run a route again and Kinetta lines it up against every earlier effort: your pace, your heart rate, and whether you're getting more pace for each heartbeat.

Ask Kit

Ask Kit about your records, with supporting numbers you can check. It’s optional, available to eligible accounts, and asks before making changes.

Works with the watch you already wear.

Apple Watch, Apple Health, Garmin Connect, Health Auto Export, Strava exports, and FIT, GPX or TCX files from almost any watch or bike computer.

Garmin Connect brings in new workouts after watch sync when activity sharing is enabled. Maps and heart rate depend on what Garmin supplies. For older history, import a Strava export in your browser or past workouts from Apple Health.

Lifting

Your previous numbers, beside every set.

Record exercises, weight and reps as you train. Compare with the previous session and follow each exercise’s history. When you finish, a summary shows your totals, any new records and each exercise beside last time.

  • Repeat a session: reuse its exercises with the previous weights and reps beside each set
  • Records: heaviest set, estimated one-rep max, most reps
  • Every exercise's history: each session, and a chart of your progress
  • Link a watch workout: include its recorded heart rate and calories in the session
See yourself improve

Your favorite loop, run after run.

Kinetta groups repeated GPS routes so you can compare pace and heart rate across similar efforts.

  • Matched routes: each repeat on one chart
  • Fitness: your pace at the same heart rate, over time
  • Similar efforts: this run next to ones like it
