Apple Health on iPhone

The iPhone beta reads the Apple Health categories you allow. It syncs when you open Kinetta; iOS can also deliver workouts in the background, with timing controlled by iOS. Apple Watch routes can be imported when available. Garmin workouts can take a while to reach Health, and Garmin does not send their GPS tracks. On a Health workout without a map, Add the map explains how to upload its original ZIP/FIT file and keep a single matching activity.

Watch files and Strava history

Upload FIT, GPX or TCX files, or your own Strava history export, from Me → Connections → Upload files.

Connected sources

Apple Health leads Me → Connections in the iPhone app. File uploads work in the browser too. Self-hosters can find intervals.icu and Health Auto Export under Advanced.

Lifting

Your previous numbers, beside every set.

Record exercises, weight and reps as you train. Compare with the previous session and follow each exercise’s history.

  • Repeat a session: reuse its exercises with the previous weights and reps beside each set
  • Records: heaviest set, estimated one-rep max, most reps
  • Every exercise's history: each session, and a chart of your progress
  • Link a watch workout: include its recorded heart rate and calories in the session
See yourself improve

Your favorite loop, run after run.

Kinetta groups repeated GPS routes so you can compare pace and heart rate across similar efforts.

  • Matched routes: each repeat on one chart
  • Fitness: your pace at the same heart rate, over time
  • Similar efforts: this run next to ones like it
