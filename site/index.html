lenkki
/ˈleŋkːi/ · Finnish · noun
  1. a jog or a walk around the block
  2. a loop
  3. a link in a chain

Lankki takes inspiration from a Finnish word associated with running, loops and links. We adapted it into our own name — a nod to moving regularly and staying connected with your people.

It started as one family's replacement for a running app and a food tracker: everything those did, kept on a computer at home, with each person's numbers private to them.

What it does

Every run, ride, walk and meal, in one place you own.

Activities

  • Upload FIT, GPX, TCX or a Strava export
  • Garmin sync via intervals.icu
  • Apple Watch sync via Health Auto Export
  • Matched routes, personal records, heatmap
  • Shoes and bikes with mileage

Food

  • USDA and Open Food Facts search
  • Barcode scanning
  • Saved meals, recipes and your own foods
  • Running calorie deficit
  • Works offline, sends when you're back

Extras

  • Optional AI coach notes (your own key)
  • Read-only SQL access to your own data
  • Installs on any phone's home screen
  • Light and dark mode
  • Miles or kilometers, per person
Private by design

Your numbers stay yours.

On your own computer

Lankki runs on a mini PC, NAS or Raspberry Pi at home. There's no Lankki company holding your data.

Private per person

Everyone has their own account. The app and the database both make sure each person sees only their own activities, food and weight.

Nothing sent out unless you choose

No ads, no tracking. Food search sends only the words you search; AI features stay off until you add your own key.

An honest note: whoever runs the server can technically read its database. Lankki is made for people who trust each other: a family, friends, a running club.

Install

Running in ten minutes.

You need a computer that stays on, with Docker installed.

01

Start it

Lankki makes up its own passwords on the first start, so there's nothing to fill in.

git clone https://github.com/BenCBanks/lankki.git
cd lankki
docker compose up -d
02

Create your account

Open http://your-computer:8790. The first account becomes the admin.

# later, to update:
git pull
docker compose up -d --build
03

Reach it from your phone

Phones need a secure address for the camera and the home screen. The connection guide covers Tailscale, a free Cloudflare Tunnel or your own domain.

Your people

Bring everyone along with a link.

  1. Create an inviteMe → People → Create invite link. Each link works once, for seven days.
  2. Send itBy text, email, or your family group chat.
  3. They pick a passwordTheir account starts in their own time zone and units, private to them.
