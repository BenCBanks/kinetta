About us

We're Ben and Rachel. We both train and care about eating well, so we built Kinetta to bring workouts, nutrition and friends together in one place.

  • ✦Training that stays readableRoutes, plans and progress together
  • ✦Food with contextMeals, portions and trends in one place
  • ✦Sharing on your termsPrivate by default, useful when shared

We use Kinetta ourselves and are still adding to it. If there's something you'd find useful, or something that isn't working, we'd like to hear from you at feedback@kinetta.app.
