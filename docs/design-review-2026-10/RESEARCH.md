# External research and what it supports

Sources inspected for the design review, 23 October 2026. Platform documentation and design-system guidance describe established patterns; studies provide evidence within their own samples. Neither proves that a particular palette will suit this owner or improve Kinetta's conversion. The proposals below are design judgments informed by those sources.

## Visual hierarchy, colour and readability

- [Carbon colour guidelines](https://www.carbondesignsystem.com/building-blocks/foundations/color/guidelines) describe layered surfaces. [Material 3 implementation guidance](https://developer.android.com/develop/ui/compose/designsystems/material3) distinguishes surface tones and elevation. **Application:** E tests white, darker figures and selective panel depth rather than changing green alone. **Limit:** guidance, not evidence that E is preferred; extra shadows may increase clutter.
- [Datawrapper on colour selection](https://www.datawrapper.de/blog/beautifulcolors) and [data-vis style guides](https://www.datawrapper.de/blog/colors-for-data-vis-style-guides) discuss restrained palettes, grey and purposeful distinctions. **Application:** fewer competing hues; data colour has a job separate from brand actions. **Limit:** neither requires all data to be grey. D/E's neutral encoding is a hypothesis and may be slower to scan.
- [Affective Color in Visualization, CHI 2017](https://www.tableau.com/research/publications/affective-color-visualization) studies emotional associations of colour in visualizations. Lower-chroma treatments can communicate calm in that setting. **Limit:** chart context and sampled associations do not establish this owner's taste, app usability or conversion. “Calm” does not mean every surface and value should be muted.
- [WCAG use of colour](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/use-of-color.html) requires information beyond colour alone. **Application:** names and values remain visible; D/E sport charts also use patterns. **Limit:** narrow patterned stacks and neutral macros still need actual readability checks.
- [WCAG resize text](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/resize-text.html), [reflow](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/reflow.html) and [focus not obscured](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/focus-not-obscured-minimum.html) inform larger-text and keyboard requirements. **Application:** responsive wrapping, visible focused fields, no clipped essential values. **Limit:** screenshots and token contrast checks cannot establish WCAG or native accessibility conformance.

## First run, tabs and empty states

- [Apple onboarding](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/onboarding) favours useful, appropriately timed introduction and setup. [Apple privacy](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/privacy) supports contextual, understandable permission requests. **Application:** optional device connection immediately after signup, a clear skip, one goals question and a later settings route. **Limit:** the proposed device-first ordering is a design judgment, not an Apple requirement or tested Kinetta improvement. Do not force Health permissions or make connection a condition of using the app.
- [HealthKit authorization](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/healthkit/authorizing-access-to-health-data) explains that an app cannot determine whether read access was denied. **Application:** say no readable data is available and offer next steps; do not assert the person denied permission.
- [Users' Understanding of Android Permissions, 2015](https://arxiv.org/abs/1504.03747) reports a 36-participant study of permission understanding. **Application:** explain why a connection is useful in context. **Limit:** older Android research is supporting context, not a Kinetta onboarding experiment or a measured signup uplift.
- [Apple tab bars](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/tab-bars) treats tabs as navigation destinations. [Android navigation bars](https://developer.android.com/develop/ui/compose/components/navigation-bar) describes three to five destinations. **Application:** test Today · Activity · Record · Progress · Food; Record opens setup/status, with an explicit Start action. **Limit:** neither establishes these exact labels or five tabs as universally optimal. Profile, connections and account settings must remain discoverable.
- [Material empty states](https://m1.material.io/patterns/empty-states.html) describes informative, actionable empty content. This is legacy Material 1 guidance, used as a supporting reference rather than a current platform specification. **Application:** replace a dominating zero chart with one relevant next action. **Limit:** retain useful empty Food and Gear actions rather than redesign every blank screen indiscriminately.

## Homepage and food logging

- [Nielsen Norman Group on page dwell time](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/how-long-do-users-stay-on-web-pages/) discusses early abandonment, drawing on [original web dwell-time analysis](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/understanding-web-browsing-behaviors-through-weibull-analysis-of-dwell-time/). **Application:** make workouts plus food understandable immediately, with a real app capture. **Limit:** older general-web evidence is not a ten-second conversion guarantee or Kinetta test result.
- [GOV.UK buttons](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/components/button/) describes clear primary-action hierarchy. **Application:** repeat one “Join the beta” action through a short homepage; keep secondary navigation quiet. **Limit:** the exact CTA wording and repetition still need review in context.
- [Log Often, Lose More, 2019](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/pdfdirect/10.1002/oby.22382) studied dietary self-monitoring in 142 adults over 24 weeks. Journaling time declined during the study and frequency was associated with weight outcomes. **Application:** reduce avoidable friction in repeated food logging. **Limit:** association does not prove fewer taps or a new visual design improves outcomes; participants and treatment context differ from Kinetta.
- [Nutrition5k, CVPR 2021](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.03375) uses measured reference meals and richer capture information to study nutrition estimation. **Application:** communicate assumptions and require review of estimated portions. **Limit:** it does not validate Kinetta's food-photo model, an arbitrary phone image or any current prompt. Follow the existing AI evaluation and explicit confirmation requirements.

## Launch and operational evidence

- [Apple App Review Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/) and [before submitting for review](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-review/before-submitting-for-review/complete-review) inform reviewer access, completeness and review notes. [Screenshot specifications](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/screenshot-specifications/) determine actual export sizes. [App privacy details](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/app-privacy-details/) describe App Store disclosures. **Application:** build a reviewer packet and privacy answers from actual behaviour after design selection. **Limit:** website images and a native privacy manifest are not completed store submission materials.
- [Garmin's public developer portal](https://developerportal.garmin.com/developer-programs/connect-developer-api) describes Activity and Health JSON and starter access. The public Starter description inspected states push-only access without historical data. **Application:** verify the app's actual program, scopes and approved rights before promising backfill or opening access. **Limit:** authenticated program documentation was not available to this review; no assertion of the account's exact entitlement or provider approval follows from a public page.
- [CISA ransomware guide](https://www.cisa.gov/stopransomware/ransomware-guide) supports protected backups and restoration checks. **Application:** off-site encrypted backups plus a proven recovery process before public launch. **Limit:** only an indexed official excerpt was accessible during research; this is not an inspection of a working backup or restore.
- [Google SRE monitoring](https://sre.google/sre-book/monitoring-distributed-systems/) discusses user-impact signals and actionable alerting. **Application:** monitor availability/errors and verify a useful alert reaches the owner. **Limit:** a dashboard, scheduled report or configured rule is not proof of alert delivery.

The practical next evidence is owner visual preference, representative task testing, physical-device accessibility, and observed launch acceptance. More colour theory cannot substitute for those checks.
