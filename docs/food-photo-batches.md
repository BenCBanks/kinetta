# Several photos for food and recipes

In Describe, Snap or Scan, tap + and choose food photos. In a recipe, tap + and photograph the ingredient's nutrition label. The photo-library picker accepts up to six images together. Review the thumbnails and remove unwanted photos before continuing; selection and removal make no provider request. Cancel the sheet to keep the existing draft, or open the picker again to replace a selection.

Food logging submits all retained views together with the description after Send. The provider is instructed to combine overlapping views, count distinct foods once and use later corrections. Review names, portions and nutrition before Add; image interpretation can still be wrong. Recipe label entry combines front/back/label views of one ingredient/package, then asks how much went in. Different packaged ingredients are added separately, so their serving sizes are not mixed. The label reader is instructed to reject photos of several different products.

The picker allows source files up to 20 MB each, compresses them locally to JPEG and bounds their combined request. The API accepts up to six JPEGs, 4 MB each and 8 MB total, and removes byte-identical duplicates. Old single-image clients still work. Errors retain the existing food/recipe draft. Requests require existing AI food access and consent; photos are not stored as account images. Provider processing/retention follows the privacy policy. The reviewed-comparison gate automatically closes for coach tools when the food-photo source fingerprint changes; this work does not bypass it.

Automated and browser checks use fictional images/provider stand-ins. A signed physical-iPhone photo-picker check remains part of release acceptance.
