# Launch feature priorities

Decision basis: the owner's approved profile/home direction, the reported food and recipe problems, the implemented feature catalog and official competitor documentation checked **29 October 2026**. This is desk research and a product hypothesis. It is not a hands-on competitor benchmark, customer-demand study or claim that Kinetta is superior.

## What the market already offers

| Product | Confirmed overlap | Implication for Kinetta |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Strava](https://www.strava.com/features) | Activity/community, training analysis, predictions, AI insights and challenges. | A feed, charts and AI do not establish an advantage by themselves. Make shared activities enjoyable and private information unmistakable. |
| [Garmin Connect+](https://support.garmin.com/en-CA/?faq=yve3hAUsxU1IEzbzo91Gt6) | Nutrition logging, macros, goals and optional activity-adjusted calorie goals within Connect. | Workouts plus food is already available elsewhere. Do not advertise that combination as unique. Availability and device requirements vary. |
| [MyFitnessPal](https://support.myfitnesspal.com/hc/en-us/articles/30332897072269-How-to-log-food-with-your-voice) | Voice entry for several foods, database matching and editable amounts. | Natural-language entry alone is insufficient. Familiar recipes, clear portions and dependable correction/review matter. |
| [MacroFactor logging](https://help.macrofactorapp.com/en/articles/215-how-to-log-food-in-macrofactor) and [check-ins](https://help.macrofactorapp.com/en/articles/247-introduction-to-check-ins-and-coaching-modules) | Batch food entry, barcode/label tools and configurable adaptive check-ins. | Reduce repeated steps. Keep target changes deliberate and explain incomplete data. |
| [Cronometer](https://cronometer.com/features/) | Detailed nutrient tracking and app/device integrations. | Do not imply database or nutrient-depth leadership. Keep sources, unknown values and manual alternatives clear. |
| [Runna](https://support.runna.com/en/articles/15443877-how-to-create-a-training-plan-in-runna) and [TrainingPeaks](https://help.trainingpeaks.com/hc/en-us/articles/204074014-Basic-vs-Premium-Athlete-Accounts) | Goal-specific plans, training summaries and deeper planning/analysis. | Put the next useful workout before retrospective detail. Avoid unverified claims of equivalent specialist coaching. |
| [Hevy](https://www.hevyapp.com/features/) | Workout logging, exercise progress and social features as its three main pillars. | Lifting must remain quick and useful within Kinetta's broader scope. Keep completed workouts easy to share. |

## What should lead

| Order | App prominence | Website prominence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Me: obvious profile identity, then **Overview / Only you**. Logged food first; current training and understandable weekly progress nearby. | Lead with workouts, food and useful progress together. Show the actual current Me screen. |
| 2 | Fast recording/import and food logging. My Recipes deliberately creates/manages recipes; logging a portion is separate. Keep search, barcode and manual tools available. | Training and Food pages show concrete tasks and current screenshots. Explain recipe saving and portion logging clearly. |
| 3 | One Activity feed, visible profiles and explicit sharing choices. Keep private health/food separate. | Friends page shows the feed and customizable shared profile, with a short explanation of privacy. |
| 4 | A current plan and clear progress: real metrics, records, lifting history and reliable comparisons. Missing data stays unknown. | Show useful analysis without promising measured fitness, perfect predictions or guaranteed results. |
| 5 | Compact Kit entry when capability, consent and release gates permit. Suggestions and icons help discovery without replacing core controls. | Describe optional help and show labeled illustrative estimates. Do not imply universal access or model accuracy evidence. |
| 6 | Recaps, milestones, small challenges and the searchable feature guide support discovery and return visits. | Keep rich detail pages and the shared guide; avoid packing every feature into the hero. |

The strongest launch hypothesis is a dependable everyday workflow for active people who currently juggle training and food tools. Its value must be demonstrated through successful first use, repeat logging, useful progress and voluntary return visits. A larger feature count is not evidence of adoption.

## Release discipline

- Keep the approved five tabs, Sky Blue controls and profile/private boundary. Improve the selected flows before adding new product areas.
- Enrollment follows the server's policy: open signup, waitlist or invitation. Account access and TestFlight installation are distinct; do not promise an App Store release or future hosted pricing.
- Saved-route planning remains deferred. Recorded maps, completed matched routes and personal heatmaps remain available.
- Use fictional demo screenshots and identify illustrative provider/device behavior. Build, regression tests, actual-device acceptance, merge and publication are separate evidence.
