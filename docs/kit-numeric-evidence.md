# Numeric facts in Kit answers

Kit's numeric guarantee is limited: every digit in its verdict and reason comes from a validated current-turn fact and is rendered by the app. The app adds the fact's own formatted value and noun/aggregation label (for example, 80 g protein per logged day). It rejects digits outside placeholders and malformed or mismatched references.

The app normalizes text with NFKC and removes Unicode format and combining marks before validation, and rejects Unicode numeric symbols (including keycap and enclosed numbers) before compatibility folding can disguise them. A placeholder may touch only approved whitespace, punctuation or connective words; the app supplies its units and label, including “per serving” for recipe serving values. Approved connecting words and hyphen/en/em-dash ranges between validated placeholders are accepted. Generic activity goal targets remain unsupported until they have an app-owned unit/label formatter. It does not verify sentence meaning or police number words. A fluent answer can still make a misleading claim around a correctly labelled value, so this validation is not a model-quality evaluation. The maintainer's reviewed live evaluation remains a separate release requirement.
