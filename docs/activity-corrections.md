# Correcting an activity summary

Open your activity, tap Edit, then Distance, duration & other details. Distance/elevation use the account's miles/feet or kilometres/metres; durations use minutes. You can correct distance, moving time, total duration, elevation gain, energy, and average/maximum heart rate. Blank explicitly means not recorded; zero distance/elevation/energy is valid. Moving time must not exceed total duration and average heart rate must not exceed maximum. Finish an active lifting session first.

Corrections change Kinetta's summary, totals, simple average pace/speed, calendar, gear totals and shared summary according to existing sharing choices. They do not write back to Apple Health, Garmin or Strava. Other members cannot edit your workout. Corrected fields remain protected when compatible Health workouts or fuller recordings arrive. Import matching uses the original distance/duration rather than your correction. Each corrected field offers Use imported …, which restores its latest compatible imported value.

The original GPS, heart-rate samples, chart and laps remain unchanged. The detail screen labels that recording separately from the corrected summary. Corrected distance or time cannot establish a new measured split or personal record: Kinetta hides splits, best efforts, matched-route membership, speed maximum, adjusted pace and affected estimates while those corrections remain. Restoring the relevant fields rebuilds record evidence from the unchanged recording and matches the route again. HR corrections hide sample-derived zones and HR estimates; ascent corrections hide affected elevation/adjusted-pace estimates. Missing effort is omitted from training-load calculations rather than made up.

The saved note for that activity is cleared after summary correction; no model is called. Historical weekly reviews already generated are dated snapshots and are not rewritten automatically. Your next requested review uses the corrected summary, subject to existing consent/access rules. A distance correction never creates a missing map, sensor recording or route. A later incompatible workout stays separate rather than being merged by a correction.
